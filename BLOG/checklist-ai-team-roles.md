<h1 align="center">Checklist: AI Management Team Roles</h1>

Use this when you are designing bots instead of one catch-all chat. One owner per lane. No client names required — only job definitions.

## Core roster

- [ ] **Chief of staff** — intakes goals, splits work by product/lane, tracks to done, surfaces one decision at a time to a human
- [ ] **Research** — web and doc lookups, competitor/tool briefs, sourced summaries (not decisions)
- [ ] **Ops / product owner(s)** — own a brand or service line; drafts and runbooks for that lane only
- [ ] **Admin / reminders** — due dates, renewals, tax calendars, “nudge one week before”
- [ ] **Final QA** — ship-ready review of drafts, PDFs, and public copy (not early brainstorming)
- [ ] **Catch-all Q&A** — off-topic questions so specialists stay focused

## Rules of engagement

- [ ] Written “done looks like” for every recurring job
- [ ] Human approve-before-send for anything under the company name
- [ ] No silent purchases, no silent customer email
- [ ] Specialists do not invent new lanes; chief routes or asks the human to hire a new role
- [ ] Weekly status: done / blocked / stalled — one short overview

## Optional later

- [ ] Sales or lead intake (form or chat first; voice only if you build it on purpose)
- [ ] Image / creative QA as a separate gate from text QA
