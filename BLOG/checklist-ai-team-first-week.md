<h1 align="center">Checklist: First Week Rollout</h1>

Keep week one boring and useful. Prove edit-and-approve on one workflow before you fan out.

## Day 1–2 — Foundations

- [ ] Name the chief of staff and write three example intakes
- [ ] Pick **one** specialist lane to prove (usually research or email drafts)
- [ ] Connect email **read + draft only** (no auto-send)
- [ ] Agree on tone: short, plain, company signature untouched

## Day 3–4 — First real work

- [ ] Run five real drafts; human edits and sends
- [ ] Log time: blank-page minutes vs edit minutes
- [ ] Fix prompts where the bot invents facts or softens hard answers
- [ ] Add a final QA pass for anything public-facing

## Day 5–7 — Expand carefully

- [ ] Add calendar reminders for one recurring due date
- [ ] Document connector scopes in a one-page runbook
- [ ] Schedule a weekly status (done / blocked / stalled)
- [ ] Decide the next lane only if week-one work is already saving hours

## Done when

- [ ] Humans trust the drafts enough to edit, not rewrite from scratch
- [ ] Nothing left the building without approval
- [ ] You know whether the subscription tier matches your usage
